Why it matters

The clothing bin isn't the answer.

Quality clothing deserves a second life.
Charity bins are overwhelmed. Sorted or not, a huge amount of what goes in never reaches anyone or is turned into rags.
You deserve something back
Quality clothing has real resale value. We'd rather put that money in your pocket than let it disappear.
What can't be resold goes somewhere meaningful
Donated to local charities including Dove Hospice, Pregnancy Choice, Red Cross, and the Salvation Army — causes close to our East Auckland home.
What we buy

We're picky, in a good way

We pay cash on the spot for what we can resell. We don't have set rates, but we have a structure to how we assess.

Most wanted
Vintage, designer & streetwear
  • Vintage & retro pieces with Character
  • Designer labels (E.g Ralph Lauren, Tommy Hilfiger or Ted Baker)
  • Streetwear labels (E.g Supreme, Stüssy or Carhartt)
  • Surf, skate and outdoor brands (E.g Rusty, Quiksilver and Patagonia)
  • Sportswear from popular teams (E.g The Warriors, All Blacks or Blackcaps)
  • Quality denim (e.g Levi's, Wrangler, Lee)
Paid individually — assessed piece by piece, even a single standout item
Also welcome
Quality everyday clothing
  • Everyday brands in good condition
  • Basics, tees & staples
  • Jackets & outerwear
  • Activewear & athleisure
  • Bulk or mixed collections
Paid per kg — one grade decision for the whole collection
Donated
Worn, damaged or fast-fashion
Kids' clothing, heavy wear, no-label
Goes to local charities and op shops — including Dove Hospice, Pregnancy Choice, Red Cross and the Salvation Army
