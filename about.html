Who we are

A mother and son,
building a better way to clear out.

Lost & Found was started by Noah and his mum, Nita, after seeing how much good clothing was ending up in charity bins during wardrobe clear-outs.

We wanted to create another option — a way for people to get something back for quality clothing they no longer want, while giving those pieces a chance to be worn again.

Noah & Nita Founders · East Auckland, NZ
Where it started

We kept seeing good clothing go into charity bins.

When people clear out their wardrobes, the easiest option is often to put everything into a charity bin and move on. But once clothing goes into a bin, the person donating it has no say in what happens next.

Some clothing is resold, some is downcycled into rags, and some doesn't make it through the sorting process. We thought there should be another option for people with quality clothing they no longer want.

The business today

A simple way to turn a wardrobe clear-out into cash.

Bring us your clothing and we'll assess it based on its condition, brand, style and resale potential. If it's something we can sell, we'll pay you for it.

The best pieces we collect find their second life through The Arkives, our online resale store. Clothing we can't resell can instead be donated through our charity network, giving it another opportunity to be useful rather than simply going to waste.

What we stand for

Quality clothing deserves a second life.

01
In circulation for longer
Good clothing shouldn't be discarded simply because its first owner has finished with it. We want to keep quality pieces in circulation for as long as possible.
02
Nothing to landfill
We assess every collection carefully. Quality pieces are resold, while clothing we can't use is directed towards local charities and op shops where possible.
03
Built in East Auckland
We're based in East Tamaki and built Lost & Found around Aucklanders. Drop-off is always welcome, and we offer free collection in selected areas.
